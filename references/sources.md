# Sources and provenance

Original synthesis of the QuantCorner Backtesting workshop: research question, pre-specified plan, integrity gate, development loop, held-out evaluation and behavior report.

- Arnott, Harvey & Markowitz, A Backtesting Protocol in the Era of Machine Learning (2018), pp. 8–15: https://ssrn.com/abstract=3275654
- Deutsche Bank, Seven Sins of Quantitative Investing (2014): https://hudsonthames.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/DB-201409-Seven_Sins_of_Quantitative_Investing.pdf
- QuantCorner, Backtest Performance Evaluation: https://www.quant-corner.com/library/robo-trade-notes/chapter-14.html
- User-supplied architectural reference, Backtesting Frameworks listing: https://mcpmarket.com/tools/skills/backtesting-frameworks-1784844668691 . Consulted for point-in-time pipelines, realistic costs, event-driven execution and walk-forward concepts. No third-party implementation or skill text copied. This package does not claim to implement all features advertised there.

The protocol and scripts here are newly authored. Efin schema observations are documented separately, with their limitations. Synthetic fixtures are generated by this package. Live smoke-test responses and source PDFs are intentionally not redistributed.
